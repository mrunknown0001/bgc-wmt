## Full description (max 4000)

```
WMT is the mobile companion to your organisation's Workload Management Tool. Keep up with your work, log your time and clear approvals away from your desk.

Sign in with the account your organisation gave you.

YOUR WORK, AT A GLANCE
• Dashboard with what is due, overdue and in progress
• My Tasks across every project you are on
• Search across projects and tasks
• Personal to-do list and notes, separate from project work
• Team leads can see their people's work from My People

TASKS AND PROJECTS
• Browse your projects, then create, edit and reassign tasks
• Start, pause and resume a task and its time is logged for you
• Ask for a time-log correction, or approve one, without leaving the app
• Fill in a project's custom fields, add tags, and follow every change in the activity history

TALK IT THROUGH
• Comment on tasks and attach files, photos or video
• Take a photo straight from a task. It can be stamped with the time and place, which is handy for site checks and proof of work.

APPROVALS
• See what is waiting for your decision and approve or reject it on the spot
• Submit requests through your organisation's own request forms and follow them to the end

STAY INFORMED
• Push notifications for assignments, comments and approvals
• Choose which notifications you get
• Bookmark or archive notifications to keep your inbox tidy

YOURS TO SET UP
• Light and dark themes with a choice of accent colours
• Sign out of your other devices from your profile

WMT needs an account on a WMT server run by your organisation. The app does not create accounts. If you do not have one, ask your administrator.
```
